# Food for Thought: Ocean Habitat, Fisheries, and Ecosystem Change

---

#### 

Fisheries are one of humanity’s oldest managed resources, yet they continue to present complex and challenging conditions. At the same time, fisheries, along with the rapidly growing aquaculture sector, can be hotbeds of controversy. Our lesson today focuses on some of the most important Canadian fisheries.

**Environmental Analysis Learning Objectives**

- Use exploratory data analysis to identify patterns, trends, and unusual observations (MSFT Programme)
- Develop and use formulas and functions in Excel and R to perform calculations and estimate quantities (e.g., catch per unit effort, population estimates).
- Apply multiple conditions to classify environmental data and identify areas or observations of interest
- Build and modify simple deterministic models

**Lesson outline**

1. Marine Sportfishing Programme (2 hours)
2. Food for Thought: Lobster fishery data (1 hour)
3. Case study #3: Rock crab fishery
4. Preparation for the field next week
