# Beneath the Surface: Measuring Animal Abundance in the Ocean

---

![](https://s3.animalia.bio/animals/photos/full/original/fish2378.webp)

The North Atlantic right whale, an iconic giant of Canada's Bay of Fundy, teeters on the brink of extinction (IUCN 2020). Once abundant, their numbers have dwindled to a few hundred due to human activities. Entanglement in fishing gear and ship strikes pose the most significant threats to their population stability. 

This class will introduce the course philosophy, structure, and layout. We will begin to practice and develop our technical skills in data manipulation, management, and visualization using a range of software packages. The class will explore the goals and objectives of conservation biology, with a particular focus on the challenges of marine conservation for highly migratory species.

**Learning Objectives**

In this lesson, we'll focus on developing the basic essential skills for importing,  manipulating and visualizing your data across Excel, R and QGIS.

- Locate and successfully import datasets into Excel, R, and QGIS using correct file formats and import procedures.
- Apply appropriate formatting techniques to ensure data is clean, consistent, and ready for analysis, including handling dates and times, and setting up data for visualization in each platform
- Aggregate and plot data within Excel using Pivot Tables and Charts
- Create simple visual representations of data in R and maps in QGIS that support initial data exploration
