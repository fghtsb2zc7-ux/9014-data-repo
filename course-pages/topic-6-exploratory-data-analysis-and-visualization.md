### National Pollutant Release Inventory

This week, we are being visited by a representative from the National Pollutant Release Inventory team. This topic's readings are all about becoming more familiar with the NPRI and how they monitor and publicly share data on pollutants. 

[1. Using and interpreting data from the National Pollutant Release Inventory - Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-pollutant-release-inventory/using-interpreting-data.html#evolution)   
Summary: NPRI has provided a great range of resources for helping to understand, access, and interpret their data. The linked page has a number of "tabs" that I would like you to move through to deepen your understanding of the NPRI data. 

Guidance:  I would like you to work through each of the tabbed sections:  
(1) About Us - Entire section with focus on main infographic and *Evolution of the NPRI* over time  
(2) Using our Data - Entire section and take good note of some of the common analysis mistakes, you can skim quickly through the specific substances listed under *Avoid double or triple counting*  
(3) Interpreting our Data - Read entire section casually, but you can skip the Waste rock components. Special focus on *Understanding changes*  
(4) Our Method - Quickly skim this section

2.  [National Pollutant Release Inventory Dashboard - Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/national-pollutant-release-inventory/tools-resources-data/all-year-dashboard.html) 

Summary: More and more organizations are making use of PowerBI dashboards and digital interfaces to communicate their findings to the public. This is a really great example of a PowerBI dashboard created by ECCC to demonstrate the powerful insights that can be gained from the NPRI dataset.  
  
Guidance:  I would recommend first taking the dashboard to full screen (). Then explore the range of interactivity throughout the dashboard.

- Explore the NPRI data -> Explore data for a community ->  Select London
  - Compare 2024 to 2014, are there any major differences in emitters?
  - Go back and select your home city or somewhere outside of London if you are from here. What types of emissions are common?  How many emitters are in your region?
- Explore the NPRI data -> Explore one year of NPRI data 
  - Click on Conventional Oil and Gas Extraction, where are the majority of those facilities located in the updated map?
  - Use the interactive pie chart to determine which sector leads in releases to air.
  - Try filtering substances to 1a, what is the leading release in this category?
  - Reset the dashboard to "Show All Data"  and then determine which sector has the highest emissions of particulate matter and then ammonia.
  - What is the leading release of the metals industry and where are producers concentrated?
- Move back to the homepage of the dashboard and select, Drill into data by location, sector, substance
  - Select Ontario, Pulp and Paper Mills, and Paper (except newsprint) Mills
  - How has this sector's emissions changed over time?
- Go back to homepage and selection Search for facility-level data
  - Search for 3M Canada Company and select the London facility.
  - Select the Release and Transfer button
  - What are the two main releases to air in 2018?
