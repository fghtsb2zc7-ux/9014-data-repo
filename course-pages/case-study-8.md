## Case Study Objectives

The objective of this exercise to create a map that helps identify areas of priority for the Thames Talbot Land Trust.

1. Identify parcels in London, Ontario that have wooded and wetland areas and areas of wetland loss.
2. In R, develop a scoring system for each parcel: Here you will be processing the data to develop a scoring system based on how you want to rank parcels and what aspects of those parcels might be important for you.
3. Create a map of the parcels you have that have the highest score! Show some R figures that demonstrate the distribution of those scores.
