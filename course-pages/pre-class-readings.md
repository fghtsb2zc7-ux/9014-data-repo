# Pre-class readings

*Module: Unit 1 Classes: Core Technical Skills > Class 4 - Biodiversity and Invasive Species Assessment*

# Pre-class work

---

In the pre-class readings you'll be learning about (1) the problem of invasive buckthorn and how to identify it, (2) our sampling protocol in the field for measuring buckthorn prevalence, (3) tick recognition and prevention.

## Pre-class readings

- [Buckthorn fact sheet](<../sources/files/02 - Invasive species monitoring/Buckthorn-Fact.Sheet (1).pdf>) - This document reviews the common species of buckthorn in the area and highlights the difference between native and invasive species here in Ontario.
  - - Focus on the impacts of buckthorn on native communities
    - Ensure you can accurately identify buckthorn in the field
- [Buckthorn sampling protocol](<../sources/files/02 - Invasive species monitoring/Buclthorn sampling.pdf>) - We will be using a systematic field sampling protocol that will allow us to measure how buckthorn changes along environmental gradients.
- [Tick Prevention](../sources/files/Lyme-Disease-Spring-Brochure-3Fold-EN-WEB.pdf) - We will be sampling areas that are off established walking paths and have a high density of leaf litter. The Canadian tick brochure will tell you about what you need to watch out for, but it is highly recommended to wear long pants.
