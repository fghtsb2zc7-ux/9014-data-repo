# Invasive Species Monitoring

---

Invasive species are one of the leading causes of biodiversity loss globally. Many environmental non-governmental organizations, municipalities, and provincial/federal governments spend significant time and financial resources on preventing or removing invasive species from the ecosystem. For our applied case study today, we will be running our own invasive species survey for buckthorn to determine focus areas for removal later in the semester.  As part of this process, we will focus on real data collection and processing. 

**Environmental Analysis Learning Objectives**

- Be familiar with standard ecological sampling techniques
- Accurately collect and digitize data for invasive species monitoring
- Conduct exploratory data analysis to determine areas to target areas of high priority for invasive species removal
