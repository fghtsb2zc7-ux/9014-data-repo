# Pollution Pathways: Tracking Canada’s Environmental Releases

---

![Smoke stack at Western University Power Plant](../sources/files/pic0_20260929130000851.jpg "Smoke stack at Western University Power Plant")

The National Pollutant Release Inventory (NPRI) is Canada’s primary tool for tracking pollutants released to air, water, and land. Each year, thousands of facilities across the country report their emissions, creating a comprehensive, publicly accessible database. In this lesson, we will meet with representatives from Environment and Climate Change Canada to learn more about the NPRI and its role in environmental monitoring. We will use the NPRI as a case study to explore how large-scale data collection supports our ability to monitor change, identify risks, and evaluate the effectiveness of management actions. This will be introduced through the group project focused on analyzing and interpreting the NPRI dataset.

**Lesson outline**

1. Group assignments and Team Charter (30 minutes)
2. Introduction to the National Pollutant Release Inventory and the German National Pollutant Monitoring (1 hour)
3. NPRI Group work (2 hours)
