
MES Team Charter - Instructions

The purpose of this charter is to help establish expectations within your group and develop a shared approach to communication, collaboration, and accountability. Keep your responses brief and practical. You may revisit and revise the charter as your team’s work develops and you should store a copy in your team’s shared folder. 

Members

Team Name:

The culture we strive towards as a team: 
What team culture do you want for your team? For example, what are your collective beliefs about collaboration, work ethic, etc., that will guide your team’s interactions and work? 
What will it look like when your team is working well? 

Game plan
Team communication/organization
How will your team primarily communicate with each other? Where and how will you organize your files? 
How will you accommodate team members’ preferences for communication and organization?

Using Teams chat and OneDrive is recommended as they are institutionally supported. 

Meetings
How often do you expect to meet? You do not need to include specific meeting dates or times in this charter.
Does anyone in your group have restrictions on the time they can meet? If a member is late, how long will you wait until the rest of the team starts the meeting? 
Will meetings normally be in person, online, or a combination? Teams should plan to meet in person when feasible.


Deadlines 
What strategies will you use to ensure deadlines are met? Will you establish internal deadlines before official due dates? What will you do if a member cannot complete their part on time? 
Will your team set internal deadlines before official due dates to allow time for reviewing, combining, and revising your work?



Decision-making and conflicts
How will your team make decisions and consider different or opposing perspectives?
How will you raise and address concerns within the team?
What steps will you take if a member repeatedly misses meetings, does not complete agreed-upon work, or stops communicating?
At what point will you seek assistance from an instructor or the Director?

Feedback
How does each member prefer to receive feedback? How will you communicate when someone’s work does not meet the team’s agreed-upon expectations? What will you do to create an environment in which respectful, constructive feedback is welcomed?

Student Initials

When completed, share the charter on your team’s shared folder and upload an initial copy to Brightspace.
